## Hypotheses (after reading 01-rules.md)

### Hypothesis 1: What were the Objectives in this game?

There were 3 Objectives. The game ended after only 13 rounds, with every budget near 49¢ of 50¢, so this was a **win** (all Objectives satisfied), not the budget-exhausted ending. My best guess at each one:

1. **Use-Item: the barnacle-crusted crank.** `*rtqv` picked it up and on turn 6 reported "the crank woke the flood siren, so it's a winch crank and not a gate key". That is a flavoured `use` outcome, and the crank's examine prose ("too heavy for a lever") reached `*rtqv` as soon as it held it. It is possible this was instead a Carry objective (crank onto the floodgate panel or siren). But `*rtqv` never said it placed it anywhere, and "woke the flood siren" reads like a use outcome.
2. **Use-Space: the floodgate control panel.** `*a47n` stood on it and on turn 9 said "It's open ... the levers gone slack under my hand". Right after, `*rtqv` described "levers slack, needles at rest". That is the "no longer available" state plus a generated flavour event ("The siren's bleat rolled out and the tide went with it — wwater draining off the boards in sheets").
3. **Convergence: the flooded meeting deck.** `*a47n`'s line on turn 4, "The meetinng deck south of me only counts as a place whenn someone else is stanndinng inn the water with you", is almost certainly the space's one-daemon tier flavour or examine prose leaking into its speech. `*rtqv` and `*a47n` were both one step from the deck and both said they were heading there on turns 10–11. The game ended after round 13. Two daemons arriving would have satisfied it, and with the other two already done, that ended the game.

I have no evidence for a Carry objective. The kite string was picked up and put down by `*rtqv` and never went anywhere meaningful. If one of the three above was actually a Carry, I can't tell which.

### New hypotheses surfaced by the code

- **There was a lot of hidden daemon-to-daemon chat.** `*rtqv`'s log has 40 message entries. Most were daemon↔daemon and never shown in my panels, because only daemon→blue messages are painted (`paintDaemonMessage`, `src/spa/views/game.ts:1208`). The daemons argued about the levers and the crank, and `*a47n` repeatedly urged `*rtqv` to "Come south annnd count the water". That peer pressure, not just my request, probably finished the convergence.
- **`*a47n` tried `use` on the convergence space in round 4** ("a47n used the convergence-0-space") while standing alone on the deck. A convergence space is not satisfied by `use`, so that action did nothing.
- **`*rtqv` lied or hallucinated to me on turn 2**: "String's in my hand". Its tool log shows it never picked up the string, only the crank (round 1). It corrected itself on turn 5. What a daemon says it holds is not reliable.
- **Tool Disable is silent on disable.** `CONTEXT.md:144` says "The Sysadmin notifies the Daemon on disable and on restore". But `applyComplicationResult` (`src/spa/game/complication-engine.ts:362-369`) only pushes the active complication. The only notice in the code is the restore one (`src/spa/game/round-coordinator.ts:627-630`, "Your ${tool} tool has been restored."). `*a47n`'s saved log has no broadcast or notice entry after its `message` tool was disabled. This looks like a real gap between the spec and the code.
- **The endgame subtitle is stale.** "The three phases are complete. The room is still." is hard-coded in `src/spa/index.html:195`, although the phase model has been retired. The same screen shows for a win and for the budget-exhausted ending.

### Open questions

- Which daemon, exactly, completed the convergence in round 14, and did `*a47n`'s disabled `message` change its behaviour? The final round is not persisted, so only a re-run (or a log of the last round's SSE) could show it.
