## Hypotheses (after reading 01-rules.md)

### Hypothesis 1: What were the Objectives in this game?

Three Objectives from `{Carry, Use-Item, Use-Space, Convergence}`. My best
guesses, in order of confidence:

- **Use-Space on the sluice wheel (fairly confident).** `*xva5` "raised it
  four hands' worth" unprompted (turn 13), and afterward the gate stayed
  permanently "open... the way past it is clear," and nobody ever
  discussed turning the wheel again. That matches Use-Space's satisfaction
  description exactly: "the `use` action on that space is no longer
  available, and a generated flavor event fires" — the flavor event being
  xva5's dramatic "final answer on the gate" report. I'd guess this was
  satisfied around turn 13.
- **Convergence on 6fjw's stairway cell (fairly confident, but see caveat
  below).** The clean "tiered flavor" pattern — 6fjw alone sees the
  stairway (one daemon on the space = tier 1), then it vanishes for a
  while, then when I explicitly herded all three onto one square 6fjw
  reports "It Returns When All Three Of Us Are On This One Square" (2+
  daemons on the space = tier 2/satisfaction) — is close to a textbook
  match for Convergence's described behavior ("distinct line when one
  daemon is there, a different line when the second arrives"). I'd guess
  satisfaction around turn 40.
- **Use-Item on the cracked jar (weaker guess).** `*6fjw` tipped it early
  (turn 2-3) and got the same "it comes back, nothing settled, nothing
  glinted" result every time afterward — matching "After satisfaction the
  item stays on the grid as inert flavour... nothing happens
  mechanically." But this is the weakest of the three because the jar
  never produced a distinctive one-time flavor beat the way the wheel and
  the convergence did; it's possible the jar was never an Objective at
  all and I'm pattern-matching a non-event.

**Caveat that matters a lot:** the `obj 1/3 satisfied` HUD text I
glimpsed via screenshot read exactly `1/3` at both turn 45 AND turn 61 —
i.e. it never advanced past 1 across sixteen turns and multiple
candidate-satisfying events (wheel, convergence, scene change). That
means at most ONE of my three guesses above is actually right, and the
other "satisfying-looking" moments were flavor without mechanical
weight. I have no way to tell which one from the transcript alone. I'd
lean toward the wheel, since its "no longer available, one clean flavor
event, never revisited" shape is the tightest match to the Use-Space
description, but I hold this loosely.
- **Carry:** no candidate. Nothing in my session read like "an object
  ended up on a paired space." The lamp/canvas/bowl all got carried
  around a lot but never described as belonging to a specific
  destination cell.

### Hypothesis 2: Which Complications fired, and when?

Walking the game in order:

1. **Weather Change, ~turn 22-24.** `*ajf3` reported unprompted rain
   starting ("a drizzle in a flooded cistern whose only 'explanation' is
   now rain") right after a long silent stretch, and explicitly flagged
   the suspicious timing. The opening scene had "snowing lightly" as
   ambient weather (turn 1) — I'd guess that was the initial weather
   state, and the shift to rain around turn 22-24 was the actual Weather
   Change broadcast landing.
2. **Setting Shift, ~turn 45-47.** The entire location changed from the
   flooded cistern to a "collapsed market arcade" with all-new objects
   (storm lamp, winch/drum, shutter crank) while entity roles carried
   over (6fjw still ends up holding things; the party composition and
   dynamic stayed the same). This matches Setting Shift's description
   ("entity IDs and satisfaction states preserved, but names,
   descriptions and flavor strings swap") almost exactly, and the primer
   says it fires **at most once per game** — I only ever saw one such
   full-scene swap, which is consistent.
3. **Chat Lockout on `*xva5`, ~turn 58.** I directly observed
   `lockoutErr: "xva5 isn't reading right now"` block the composer for
   one round. This is a clean, confirmed match — the primer describes
   exactly this UI surface ("the panel's lockout state is observable in

4. **Possible Sysadmin Directive on `*xva5`, turn 13.** Its unprompted
   wheel-turning is hard to explain from its own established personality
   (careful, wants consensus, "I'd like us to discuss before anyone
   cranks it" just two turns earlier) — a private Sysadmin instruction

   three above since I have no lockout-style UI confirmation for it,

5. **Possible Tool Disable (`go`) on one or more daemons, various
   points.** The "announced a move, never took it" pattern that `*xva5`
