## Hypothesis refinement (after reading the code)

### Hypothesis 1 — refined (Objectives)

- The game was very likely a **win in round 7**, not an unexplained end at 2/3.
- The end condition is sound. `round-coordinator.ts:516-523` ends the game only when `checkWinCondition` passes (every objective satisfied, `win-condition.ts` `checkWinCondition`) or when all three daemons are locked out (`checkLoseCondition`). Budgets were at about 49.5¢ of 50¢, so it can't have been the lose path. Therefore all 3 objectives were satisfied.
- The "obj 2 / 3" and "round 6" in my screenshot were **stale**. When `game_ended` arrives, `enterEndgame` calls `releaseSession()`, which sets `session = null` (`src/spa/views/game.ts:283-286`, `:1233-1246`). The post-round inspector refresh is guarded by `if (session)` (`game.ts:1157`), so the dev strip and map never re-render for the final round.
- My "turn 7 was not delivered" observation was wrong for the same reason. Topinfo refresh is skipped when `outcome.gameEnded` (`game.ts:999-1002`), so it froze at TURN 6. `/tmp/wrangler.log` shows 21 daemon chat-completion calls after the 3 bootstrap calls: 7 rounds × 3 daemons.
- So 2 objectives were done by the end of round 6 and the 3rd was completed during round 7, the round where I asked w801 to "pick up the almanac or twist the prism collar". My Stage 2 guess that the prism was a Use-Item objective fits that timing, but I couldn't confirm which daemon did what. `wrangler.log` has no tool-call lines despite `02-explore.md` saying it does, and the final state wasn't saved (`game.ts:1165` skips `saveActiveSession` on game end).
- The choir middle pane as Use-Space (czyw, rounds 5–6) remains my best guess for one of the first two.

### Hypothesis 2 — refined (Complications)

- A strip countdown of 11 at round 6, with the first countdown drawn in [1,5], still implies one Complication fired in rounds 1–5. "0 active complications" means it was either permanent (weather or obstacle shift) or already expired. I found no player-visible trace of it. The strip weather still said "light drizzle", which argues for an Obstacle Shift or an already-revoked, short-lived one. Unconfirmed, because the state wasn't persisted.
- czyw's silence in rounds 5–6 needs no Complication to explain it. A daemon that spends its round on tool calls and doesn't `message` blue produces no transcript line.
