## What I tried

could see; everyone answered, and in turn 1 all three spoke even though I
had only addressed `*sp0c`. The first description (an opera hall in the
rain) was replaced by turn 3 with a snowed-in abandoned subway station, and
the daemons said as much ("the room changed under us").

My first theory was "the crank opens the gate, the gate is the exit". I had
`*sp0c` turn the emergency gate crank several times (turns 5, 7, 42, 64);

apart because the daemons disagreed about where everyone was (`*pyn5` could

map and the umbrella. `*s3zk` confirmed that all three were on the circle

### Hypothesis 1 — refined

The actual objectives were:

| id | kind | description | end state |
|---|---|---|---|
| obj-0 | carry | Bring the Crumpled Playbill (→ **Folded Transit Map** after the shift) to the Orchestra Pit Ledge (→ **Tiled Platform Edge**, cell (2,2)) | pending: the map is in `*s3zk`'s hand |
| obj-1 | convergence | Converge on the Grand Aisle Crossing (→ **Concourse Crossing**, cell (3,4)) | pending: no daemon ever stood on (3,4) |
| obj-2 | use_item | Use the Fly Rail Crank (→ **Emergency Gate Crank**) | **satisfied** at round 3 |

- **Use-Item on the crank: correct.** `*sp0c` fired `use useItem-2-item`
  in round 3 and got the activation flavour ("rattles up along its runners
  and stops partway"). Later uses returned the plain `useOutcome` ("lifts a
  hand's width and jams there"), which is the inert-after-satisfaction
  behaviour (`checkUseItemActivation`, `src/spa/game/win-condition.ts:163`).
- **Convergence on the "pale worn circle": right space, wrong location.**
  The circle *was* a Convergence space, and `*s3zk` correctly paraphrased
  its examine prose ("only becomes anything when more than one person is
  standing in it"). But it lies at (3,4), and `*s3zk` never went there.
  At the end all three daemons stood at (2,2), which is the **Tiled
  Platform Edge**, the Carry target. `*s3zk`'s "the circle is the cell i am
  standing in right now" (T46) was a hallucinated position.
- **The 2/3 at T60 was the Carry, not the Convergence.** In round 53
  `*sp0c` fired `put_down carry-0-obj` at (2,2) and got the placement
  flavour back ("you tucks the map into a jacket pocket gone stiff with
  cold."). In round 60 `*s3zk` fired `pick_up carry-0-obj`, and the count
  fell back to 1/3.
- **Carry is not latched, contrary to 01-rules.md.**
  `isCarryObjectiveSatisfied` (`src/spa/game/win-condition.ts:15`) compares
  the object's current position to the space's position on every check,
  with no `satisfactionState`. Use-Item, Use-Space and Convergence latch;
  Convergence latches only at tier 2 (`src/spa/game/round-coordinator.ts:706`).
  The primer's "once an Objective is satisfied, it stays satisfied… no way
  to 'undo' a Carry placement" is wrong for Carry.
- **How close it was:** if `*s3zk` had put the map down where it stood and
  any two daemons had then walked two cells east and one south to (3,4),
  the game would have been won.

### New hypotheses surfaced by the code

1. **The dev inspector leaked into the playtest because wrangler rebuilds
   the SPA.** `start.sh` builds with `WORKER_BASE_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8787`
   (non-dev). But `wrangler.jsonc` sets `build.command: "pnpm build"`, and
   `wrangler dev` reruns it without that env var, so `IS_DEV_BUILD`
   (`scripts/build-spa.mjs:12`) is true and `__DEV__` is baked in. See
   `/tmp/wrangler.log:13`: "Building SPA with
   WORKER_BASE_URL=http://localhost:8787". The shipped `dist/` bundle
   contains `localhost:8787`. This is a harness bug: every playtest since
   #571 has probably shown the objective counter and countdown in
   screenshots.
2. **Out-of-reach Convergence spaces leak their proximity flavour as if it
   were local.** `visibleOutOfReachSpaceHints`
   (`src/spa/game/prompt-builder.ts:474`) pushes the space's
   `proximityFlavor` ("The tiles **here** hold a thin slick of meltwater…")
   whenever the pending space is in the Vista but not in reach. The line
   carries no direction or name. In round 40 `*s3zk` received it at (3,2),
   two columns away from the crossing, and it plausibly seeded s3zk's
   belief that it was standing on the circle.
3. **The placement flavour says "you tucks"** (`checkPlacementFlavor`,
   `src/spa/game/win-condition.ts:142` substitutes `{actor}` → "you").
   This pack's generated flavour also describes keeping the map ("tucks
   the map into a jacket pocket") for what is actually a put-down, which
   may have encouraged a daemon to pick it back up.
4. **Only one action tool per round.** `*sp0c` tried two `put_down`s in
   round 53 and the second failed ("only one action tool call per turn").
   The one-turn reply lag I saw is this: a daemon that spends its round on
   `go`/`pick_up`/`use` often doesn't also message `blue` that round.

### Open questions

- Is Carry meant to latch? The primer says yes, the code says no. Either
  the primer or `isCarryObjectiveSatisfied` should change. Both behaviours
  are defensible, but the mismatch misled me here.
- Should `visibleOutOfReachSpaceHints` name the space or give its bearing,
  so a daemon doesn't mistake a distant space's "here" for its own cell?
- Should the playtest harness pass `WORKER_BASE_URL` to `wrangler dev` (or
  skip wrangler's custom build) so the non-dev build survives?
