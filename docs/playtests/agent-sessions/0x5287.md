### Hypothesis 1 — refined

The objectives were **Carry + Use-Space + Convergence**, and the mitten and map were decoys. I'm now much more confident.

- **Convergence on the bench / drying rack.** The CONVERGENCE space's examineDescription "MUST hint that shared occupancy … matters — e.g. \"a meeting place\"" (`src/spa/game/content-pack-provider.ts:31`). 162m's T1 line, "only turns into a meeting place when somebody else sits down", is almost the rule's example text. Convergence latches `satisfied` once tier 2 is reached, meaning two daemons on the space cell (`src/spa/game/round-coordinator.ts:706-714`, tier counting in `src/spa/game/win-condition.ts:44-66`). The Setting Shift keeps entity IDs, so the rack is the same entity as the bench. 162m's "so the rack finally has its second body" (T56) is almost certainly the satisfying moment, and very likely the last objective, since the endgame followed within about 2 rounds.
- **Use-Space on the Rusted Signal Console.** The USE_SPACE examineDescription "MUST contain at least one activation/use cue word", and the list includes "operate", "lever" and "console" (`content-pack-provider.ts:28`). 162m's T2 line, "one lever rusted stiff. Patience would still operate it", contains all three. The T9 line, "the dials glow steady amber and a row of bulbs is stuttering down the platform edge", fits the required `activationFlavor` ("1 sentence, world third-person"). The first pull at T4 did nothing, probably because the console wasn't yet in 162m's cell or front arc.
- **Carry: Tarnished Token Pouch → Fare Booth Ledge.** The CARRY object's examineDescription "MUST reference the paired space by name" (`content-pack-provider.ts:24`), and that fits "its label reads as belonging on a fare booth ledge". The check is purely positional: object cell == space cell (`win-condition.ts:15-31`). adr1's early drops "landed on cold tile" because it was one cell away. The T33 drop on the ledge's own tile satisfied it.
- **Decoys: the Lone Wool Mitten and the Crumpled Transit Map.** Every pack has exactly 2 decoys, whose examine prose "MUST NOT contain any activation/use-cue keyword … identifiable by lack of tell" (`content-pack-provider.ts:36-37`). That matches "no label, no stamp … the map names nowhere at all" and "no label, no name, no initials". After the shift they became the rag and the composing stick. So my Stage 1 theory, "mitten on the bench triggered the room change", was wrong twice over: the mitten was a decoy, and the room change was a scheduled Setting Shift. adr1 and 162m spent many rounds (T37–T58) and I spent many prompts moving decoys around. The win came only because moving them happened to put two daemons on the Convergence space.

Lockout, directive and tool-disable durations are 3–5 rounds (lines 32-34). ### New hypotheses surfaced by the code

- **The endgame copy is stale.** "The three phases are complete. The room is still." is hardcoded in `src/spa/index.html:195`. The primer says the three-phase structure was retired, so every win (and every loss, since the screen is shared) tells the player about three phases they never saw. It misled me in Stage 1 into hunting for a third room.
- **Carry is not latched.** The primer says "Once an Objective is satisfied, it stays satisfied". But `checkWinCondition` re-evaluates Carry from live positions every round (`win-condition.ts:74-76`), while Use-Item, Use-Space and Convergence read a latched `satisfactionState`. If a daemon had picked the pouch back up after T33, the Carry objective would have come undone. Either the primer or the code is out of step.
- **Silent turns are real actions.** The daemon that goes quiet after a concrete instruction has most likely spent its round on `go` / `put_down` without a `message` to blue. The action lands, and the chat report arrives a round later, which explains the "one turn late" pattern.
- **Decoys are a strong trap for the player.** The decoys' flat, tell-less prose ("names nowhere at all") actively prompts the player *and* the daemons to invent destinations for them: map → console, mitten → bench. About a third of my session went into decoy logistics.

- Is the non-latched Carry intended, and the primer wrong, or is it a bug?
